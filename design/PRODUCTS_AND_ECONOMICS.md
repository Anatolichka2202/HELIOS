# Product and business strategy

HELIOS is a game about industrial and societal expansion across the whole Solar System. Venus and Mercury are scenarios, not the game boundary.

**Dream:** long-term, high-fidelity PC strategy with narrative campaign, configurable sessions, engineering-inspired logistics, and cinematic presentation.

**Commercial hypothesis:** Lite can be distributed faster, gather player feedback and validate core gameplay; Mobile can become a touch-first standalone product. PC remains a long-term premium product.

No revenue or retention has been validated. Monetization choices are hypotheses until real player tests. Avoid pay-to-win and coercive mechanics.

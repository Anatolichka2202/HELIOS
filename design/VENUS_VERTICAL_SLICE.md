# Venus vertical slice acceptance criteria

- Start screen offers **Venus Campaign** and **Venus Session**.
- Map depicts Venus and its atmospheric operational layers; do not present eight colonizable planets in this slice.
- First systems: research, finance, power, atmospheric hazards, aerostat deployment, Earth–Venus supply shipments.
- Campaign begins in Russia with fictional USSR secret-program archival hook; international cooperation is a later decision.
- Session starts with configurable resources/objective and bypasses story gating.
- A mission can fail through depleted budget, missed logistics, or engineering hazards; clear feedback and recovery options.
- Save/load; mobile-friendly UI; accessibility and manual iPhone test.
- All simulation assumptions documented and labeled; no claim of realistic full terraforming in short time.
- Keep shared solar-system data architecture extensible beyond Venus.

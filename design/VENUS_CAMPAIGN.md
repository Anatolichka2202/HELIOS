# HELIOS: VENUS — Campaign vertical slice

## Scope
The HELIOS franchise spans the Solar System. **The first playable campaign and session mode focus on Venus only.** Other planets may appear as sources of logistics, politics and narrative, but are not playable development regions in this milestone.

## Logline
An exceptionally talented, strikingly handsome young engineer, dismissed as a madman, uncovers the legacy of a classified Soviet Venus initiative. Starting in Russia, he revives its abandoned ambitions and attempts to build a viable human future above Venus's clouds. As the project scales, it becomes an international endeavor, with competing visions for the planet's fate.

## Historical framing
The secret USSR Venus initiative and its characters are **fictional alternate-history elements**, not assertions about a real historical program. Soviet Venera missions are historical inspiration, not proof of a secret colonization project.

## Narrative pillars
1. Vision versus evidence: ambition is tested by physics and engineering, not rewarded by fiat.
2. Inheritance: Soviet-era archives, institutional memory, aging infrastructure and political obligations.
3. Russia to international: initial Russian research and industrial program must secure outside scientific and financial cooperation; alliances carry trade-offs.
4. Beauty and isolation: a charismatic protagonist with genuine personal flaws and relationships, not a flawless savior.
5. Multiple futures: floating settlements, atmospheric industry and optional long-term terraforming research; no mandatory full terraforming.

## Campaign opening: "The impossible dossier"
- **Prologue:** archival fragment from the closed Soviet initiative; the actual historical extent is a mystery.
- **Act I — Russia:** young engineer pitches the Venus revival, is publicly dismissed; a small team secures partial funding and retrieves technical archives.
- **Act II — Cloudline:** design and validate a robotic aerostat demonstration in Venus's temperate-altitude cloud layer; establish communications, power and corrosion mitigation.
- **Act III — International:** industrial partners and research agencies join or oppose; player negotiates access, ownership, safety and science.
- **Act IV — Permanent presence:** supply chains, floating habitats, manufacturing and political institutions emerge.
- **Long horizon:** investigate planetary-scale transformation as a speculative multi-century program with explicit energy, mass and timescale constraints.

## First playable gameplay loop
Read mission brief → allocate budget/research/engineering effort → develop and test atmospheric probe → establish aerostat → keep supply and power stable → negotiate international cooperation → decide next industrial investment.

## Session mode
Same Venus model and infrastructure rules, without mandatory story scenes. Adjustable starting budget, technology, atmospheric hazards, and objectives. No multiplayer implementation promised in this milestone.

## Narrative questions to decide later
Protagonist's name, birth year, exact timeline, relationship to original Soviet initiative, and the extent of alternate history are deliberately open. Avoid inventing canon before approval.
